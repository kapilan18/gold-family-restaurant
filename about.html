Our Heritage & Story

The Gold Restaurant Journey

From time-honored recipes to a landmark of hospitality in Pudukkottai.

Serving With Honor Since Day One

Welcome to Gold Family Restaurant

Gold Restaurant was born out of a lifelong passion for culinary perfection. Situated conveniently at Hotel Sivalaya, opposite the iconic RKP Theatre in Pudukkottai, our restaurant has earned its reputation as the benchmark for authentic biryanis, tandoori grills, and memorable family dining.

Our culinary philosophy is simple: never cut corners on quality. We source the finest Seeraga Samba and long-grain Basmati rice, farm-fresh poultry and meats, pure dairy ghee, and whole spices ground fresh in our kitchen each morning.

From our celebrated Royal Mutton Dum Biryani to our signature Egyptian Charcoal BBQ, every dish is an artful balance of aromatics, smoky depth, and comforting warmth.
